#### 6.2 Most Important Edge Cases Verified Across Subsystems

1. **Evidence Ledger & Provenance Edge Cases (`span-validation.test.ts`, `two-stage-span-gate.test.ts`, `ledger.test.ts`):**
   - Plausible-but-wrong numeric values (`"CA 15-3 was 42"` vs span `"CA 15-3 was 32 U/mL"` → rejected at Stage A).
   - Valid span quote paired with contradicted structured `FactValue` (`quote: "Hemoglobin: 11.2 g/dL"` with `valueText: "6.2"` or `"999.99"` → rejected at Stage B with `value_not_entailed`).
   - Cross-page (`page-1` + `page-2`) and cross-document (`doc-1` + `doc-2`) span linking attempts → rejected with `cross_page` / `cross_document`.
   - Anonymous human correction (`extractorKind: 'human', createdBy: null`) → rejected with `unauthorized_correction`.
   - Human correction preserves original `evidence_fact` row `A` (`state = 'corrected'`) while inserting replacement row `B` (`corrects_fact_id = A.id`, `state = 'verified'`) in a single transaction.
2. **Contradiction Detection & Reconciliation Edge Cases (`conflict.test.ts`, `longitudinal-conflicts.test.ts`, `reconciliation-regression.test.ts`):**
   - Multi-date serial labs (`2026-06-02` vs `2026-07-26`) vs same-day conflicting labs (`2026-07-26` `10.2` vs `13.8 g/dL`) — older serial observation is kept out of the same-day conflict set.
   - Leap-year and invalid calendar dates (`2024-02-29` valid vs `2026-02-30` invalid → `incomparable`, never silently equated).
   - Mismatched lab units (`U/mL` vs `kU/L`) and omitted medication doses → flagged as `incomparable` for human review rather than `agree`.
   - Reopening a settled record (`DEMO-002`): when a newly uploaded document contradicts a previously `verified` appointment fact after a `consultation_packet` was already approved, both facts transition to `conflicting` and the approved packet is flagged `ledgerAdvanced = true` (stale).
3. **OPD Queue, Concurrency & Synchronization Edge Cases (`slot-optimizer.test.ts`, `opd-flow.test.ts`, `wave1-governance-and-sync.test.ts`):**
   - Attempting to offer a vacant OPD slot to a waitlisted patient with `readinessBand = 'gaps'`, `'blocked'`, an open conflict, or a revoked source document → `0` candidates returned.
   - Priority tag `expedited_slot_requested` without audit provenance (`priorityProvenance: null`) → downgraded to `routine_followup` so unanchored urgency claims cannot jump the queue.
   - Concurrent double-offer on the same vacant slot → blocked by `FOR UPDATE` row lock and partial unique index `idx_opd_waitlist_one_active_offer_per_slot`.
   - Accepting an OPD slot offer (`acceptOpdSlotOffer`) enforces `offer_expires_at > now()` and transactionally synchronizes Milestone B `appointment` and `appointment_status_history`.
4. **Security, Privacy & Consent Withdrawal Edge Cases (`remediation-0020.test.ts`, `packet-withdrawal-review.test.ts`, `demo-safety.test.ts`, `middleware-security.test.ts`):**
   - Querying any of the 63 forced-RLS tenant tables when `app.org_id` is unset or set to empty string `''` → returns `0` rows without throwing a PostgreSQL UUID syntax error (`NULLIF(current_setting('app.org_id', true), '')::uuid`).
   - Attempting `pnpm demo:reset` when `NODE_ENV=production` or when a sentinel non-demo organization/patient exists in the database → aborts immediately with `demo_reset_requires_development` / mixed-data refusal, preserving all rows (`demo-safety-integration.test.ts`).
   - Withdrawing consent on a source document *after* a Consultation Packet or Studio draft has already been approved and frozen → subsequent HTTP exports (`markdown`, `json`, `abdm`, `mcode`), HIP transfers, and TTS calls fail closed with `HTTP 409 Conflict`.

### Section 12 — Judge Questions and Evidence-Based Answers

Below are **15 tough, realistic questions** Health-a-thon 2026 judges (oncologists, AI researchers, NHA/ABDM architects, and software quality engineers) are likely to ask, paired with concise, defensible answers grounded strictly in repository evidence.

#### Q1: *"You report 1,202 unit/integration tests and 102 Playwright E2E tests passing at 100%. Does that mean your AI is 100% accurate on real oncology documents?"*
- **Answer:** No—and we explicitly separate **software & safety-gate verification** from **real-world clinical document accuracy**. Our `1,202` unit/DB tests and `102` Chromium E2E tests prove that our deterministic two-stage provenance gate (`validateSpans` + `validateCandidateProvenance`), state machine (`136` cases), RBAC matrix (`240` cases), conflict comparators (`33` cases), and PostgreSQL RLS/hash-chain invariants work as engineered on synthetic fixtures. Real-world OCR Character Error Rate (CER) and extraction Precision/Recall on degraded, skewed, or handwritten hospital scans have not been benchmarked on patient data yet and are the primary target of our proposed 90-day NCG shadow pilot.

#### Q2: *"How do you prevent an LLM from citing a real PDF span while hallucinating or flipping a lab value or surgery date?"*
- **Answer:** Every extracted candidate must pass a **Two-Stage Deterministic Provenance Gate (`packages/domain/src/provenance/span-validation.ts`)** inside `insertFact()` (`packages/db/src/services/ledger.ts:226-265`) before it can enter `evidence_fact`:
  1. **Stage A (`validateSpans`):** Confirms the linked `text_span` IDs exist on the same page of the same document and that `verbatimQuote` is an exact normalized contiguous substring of the concatenated OCR spans.
  2. **Stage B (`validateCandidateProvenance`):** Unpacks all **9 `FactValue` union types** (`date`, `appointment`, `lab`, `identifier`, `medication`, `procedure`, `facility`, `cycle`, `text`) and verifies that the structured value, numeric token, unit, or ISO date is deterministically entailed by the `verbatimQuote`.
  - In `span-validation.test.ts` (`24/24`) and `two-stage-span-gate.test.ts` (`6/6`), **100% (`12/12`)** of candidates with valid quotes but contradicted values (e.g., quote `"Hemoglobin: 11.2 g/dL"` paired with `valueText: "6.2"` or `"999.99"`) are rejected with `value_not_entailed`.

#### Q3: *"What happens if an uploaded scan is illegible, blank, or handwritten in a script local OCR can't read?"*
- **Answer:** OncoBrief **quarantines unreadable documents rather than guessing**. When `LocalMultimodalOcrAdapter` or `SarvamVisionOcrAdapter` extracts zero valid text spans (tested in `ocr-correctness.test.ts` with a blank raster PNG and corrupt binary buffers after removing Strategy C raw-byte ASCII scraping in `TASK-02.03`), `ingestDocument()` sets `document.ingest_status = 'quarantined'` and `ingest_error = 'manual_transcription_required'`, writing **0 fabricated facts** to the ledger and surfacing a clear quarantine badge on `/patients/[id]/sources`.

#### Q4: *"Why did earlier versions of OncoBrief flag serial blood tests as contradictions, and how did you verify the fix?"*
- **Answer:** In V1–V2, `slotKey` grouped all `lab_result.as_written` facts by `lab:<name>` regardless of observation date, causing serial CBC Hemoglobin values (`11.4 g/dL` on `2 June 2025` vs. `10.2 g/dL` on `26 July 2025` in `DEMO-001`) to be flagged as a contradiction (`100%` false-conflict rate on dated trends). In V4 (`packages/domain/src/conflict/comparators.ts:154-258`), we introduced `isLongitudinalFactType()` and `observationVerdict()`: observations on distinct calendar dates return `'serial'` (`'agree'`) and populate the `LongitudinalLabFlowsheet` (`Δ = -1.2 g/dL`), whereas two divergent values on the **same calendar date** (`10.2` vs `13.8 g/dL` on `2026-07-26`) still return `'disagree'` and open a conflict. This is verified by **33 / 33** unit tests in `conflict.test.ts` and `longitudinal-conflicts.test.ts`.

#### Q5: *"Does your Longitudinal Lab Flowsheet or OPD Waitlist Optimizer violate the hackathon's non-diagnostic / no-triage boundary?"*
- **Answer:** No.
  - **Lab Flowsheet (`longitudinal-review.ts`):** Computes only the pure arithmetic difference (`latestNumeric - previousNumeric`, e.g., `-1.2 g/dL`) and displays source-reported text verbatim—it never labels a value `"abnormal"`, `"critical"`, `"toxic"`, or `"progressive disease"`.
  - **OPD Waitlist Optimizer (`slot-optimizer.ts`):** Ranks waitlisted candidates strictly by **administrative consultation readiness** (`readiness_band === 'ready'`, zero open conflicts, valid document consent, clinic/date match) plus provenance-anchored administrative scheduling tags (`expedited_slot_requested` with staff audit provenance, followed by FIFO requested date). It never computes a medical risk score or clinical acuity triage. Both modules pass `pnpm boundary:check` (`0` violations across `248` source files).

#### Q6: *"How did you measure the '45+ clicks reduced to 2 clicks' claim?"*
- **Answer:** That is a direct UI interaction measurement on our 10-document referral fixture (`DEMO-001`), backed by `packages/db/test/batch-verification.test.ts` (`4/4`) and Playwright `independent-judge-journey.spec.ts` Scene 3. In the row-by-row interface, verifying 23–26 extracted facts required opening and confirming each row individually (`2 clicks × 23..26 = 46..52 clicks`). With `BatchVerificationModal` and `verifyEvidenceBatch()`, the clinician clicks **`Batch Verify High-Confidence Facts`** (Click 1), reviews the pre-filtered `>= 0.90` confidence non-conflicting facts with their verbatim quotes, and clicks **`Confirm Batch Verification`** (Click 2), verifying all **26 facts** in one ACID transaction. We explicitly label this as a verified UI click reduction on synthetic fixtures, not a clinical time-motion study.

#### Q7: *"What happens if a batch verification request includes even one low-confidence or conflicting fact?"*
- **Answer:** The entire batch is rejected atomically (`BatchVerificationError`), and **zero** facts change state. In `packages/db/test/batch-verification.test.ts`, we test passing `[eligibleFactId, conflictFactId]`, passing a fact with `confidence_raw = 0.65 < 0.90`, and passing a fact from another patient (`p3` under `p1`). All three throw `BatchVerificationError` before any `ledger_entry` is written, leaving `eligibleFactId` in `'extracted'` state.

#### Q8: *"Have real oncologists, nurses, or patients tested OncoBrief?"*
- **Answer:** Outside our team's Doctor Partner (**Bhoomi Goel**, who co-designed the clinical workflows and reviewed the synthetic oncology journeys alongside our three engineering leads), **no external hospital clinicians or real patients (`N = 0`) have participated in a formal usability study yet**, because the hackathon Build Sprint strictly prohibits using real patient data. All usability testing to date consists of internal doctor-partner review and multi-persona walkthroughs across 5 seeded hospital roles (`Clinician`, `Coordinator`, `Records Officer`, `Admin`, `Auditor`), which drove 12 verified UX and safety repairs. External clinical usability (`SUS` target `>= 75`) and shadow-pilot validation (`N = 100` referrals) are our immediate Phase-2 pilot steps.

#### Q9: *"Which external AI and healthcare integrations are running live versus simulated in your evaluation?"*
- **Answer:** Every integration port reports its exact runtime verification tier via `GET /api/v1/system/providers` and `<ProviderModeBadge />`:
  - **Executed & Verified Locally (100% Real Code):** PostgreSQL 16/17 RLS & append-only triggers, SHA-256 hash chains, two-stage span validation, local PDF/DICOM/FHIR/vCard + PNG `IDAT` pixel OCR (`LocalMultimodalOcrAdapter`), `RuleBasedExtractor` (English, Hindi, Tamil), Fidelius `ECDH + HKDF-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM` crypto (`abdm-crypto.ts`), OPD slot optimizer, and Studio draft generators.
  - **Live Cloud Verified Historically:** AWS Mumbai (`ap-south-1`) pipeline (`S3 → SQS → Lambda → Textract → Bedrock Nova Pro`, verified Sept 25, 2026 in `docs/final-submission-verification.md`) and `Sarvam Bulbul v3` TTS (`HTTP 200 OK` returning `22050 Hz` PCM WAV in `ONCOBRIEF_RELEASE_BLOCKERS.md`).

#### Q10: *"In your Oct 30 internal Sarvam audit, you found a bug where all Indic TTS/STT calls collapsed to `en-IN`. Is that fixed and verified now?"*
- **Answer:** Yes—completely fixed and verified by 46 automated tests in Phase 01 (`ANTIGRAVITY_PHASE_01A_REPORT.md` & `ANTIGRAVITY_PHASE_01B_REPORT.md`). Previously, `normalizeLanguageCode()` in `packages/adapters/src/sarvam-voice.ts` called `code.toLowerCase()` (`'ta-IN' → 'ta-in'`) before checking `SUPPORTED_LANGUAGES` (`'ta-IN'`), causing every non-English locale to fall back to `'en-IN'`. We repaired `normalizeLanguageCode()` to canonicalize all 11 BCP-47 locales, mapped Odia `'or-IN'` to Sarvam's provider code `'od-IN'` via `toSarvamApiLanguageCode()`, implemented `mergeWavBase64Chunks()` to strip duplicate 44-byte RIFF headers across multi-chunk TTS responses, and wired `resolveLocalizedMessageText()` so selecting Tamil (`ta-IN`) displays and synthesizes the verified Tamil script reminder (`sarvam-voice-contracts.test.ts` `15/15`, `tts-scope.test.ts` `17/17`, `phase5-continuity-voice.spec.ts` `4/4`).

#### Q11: *"How can an append-only, immutable Evidence Ledger comply with a patient's right to revoke consent under ABDM or the DPDP Act?"*
- **Answer:** We separate **cryptographic audit-chain linkage** from **source content accessibility**:
  1. When a patient revokes ABDM consent (`purgeConsentData` in `abdm-hiu.ts`), OncoBrief deletes raw storage objects, wipes the ephemeral X25519 private key (`ephemeral_private_key_enc = NULL`), and sets `document.ingest_error = 'consent_revoked_or_expired'`.
  2. Migration `0010_abdm_revoked_source_access.sql` applies a `RESTRICTIVE` PostgreSQL RLS policy (`abdm_source_access`) across `evidence_fact`, `text_span`, `document_page`, `extraction_candidate`, and `timeline_event`, so `oncobrief_app` immediately sees `0` rows from that document without deleting `ledger_entry` rows or breaking the SHA-256 chain.
  3. Even if a Consultation Packet or Studio document was approved *before* revocation, `assertPacketSourcesAccessible` and `assertStudioArtifactSourcesAccessible` block all 7 export and TTS endpoints with **`HTTP 409 Conflict`** (verified in `independent-judge-journey.spec.ts` Test 2).

#### Q12: *"Is your AI Assistant a true autonomous multi-step agent, or a deterministic state machine?"*
- **Answer:** It is intentionally a **Bounded Deterministic State-Graph (`WorkflowStateGraph<TState>` in `packages/domain/src/orchestration/workflow-graph.ts`) with Single-Turn LLM Synthesis and Staged Human Confirmation Gates**, not an unconstrained autonomous agent. In healthcare operations, allowing an LLM to autonomously loop and mutate database records is a safety hazard. Instead, our four state graphs (`DocumentIngestion`, `AssistantQuery`, `OpdBackfill`, and `ContinuityOutreach`) execute explicit, auditable nodes (`safety_and_intent_gate → plan_subgoals_node → retrieve_sources_node → propose_action_gate → grounded_synthesis_node → citation_verification_gate`), persist their `execution_trace_json` to PostgreSQL (`0020_remediation_rls_and_governance.sql`), and halt at `propose_action_gate` (`status: 'awaiting_human_approval'`) until a clinician clicks confirm.

#### Q13: *"What happens if a clinician edits a generated letter in Productivity Studio and accidentally changes a lab value or adds an uncited diagnosis?"*
- **Answer:** Saving or approving the draft is rejected by `revalidateStudioMarkdownCitations()` (`packages/domain/src/studio/draft-generator.ts`). In `studio-safety-audit.test.ts` (`13/13`), we verify that changing `"Hemoglobin: 10.8 g/dL [E3]"` to `"19.9 g/dL [E3]"` or `"110.8 mg/dL [E3]"`, adding `"New diagnosis: lung cancer"`, or inserting `"Start chemotherapy tomorrow"` fails validation (`[E3]:ungrounded_value` / `boundary_violation`). One honest trade-off of this strict exact-clause validator is that clinicians cannot freely paraphrase a cited clause in their own words—a limitation we disclose and plan to refine in our pilot.

#### Q14: *"Slide 8 of your build-sprint deck mentions a 'Patient Mobile App'. Is that part of the verified evaluation?"*
- **Answer:** No—and we are completely transparent about its status. The patient mobile companion (`apps/mobile` on branch `oncobrief-patient-app`, audited in `docs/audits/PATIENT_MOBILE_BRANCH_AUDIT.md`) is an isolated offline Expo/React Native prototype (`36` unit tests, `5` web E2E tests) that is **not yet integrated with the `@oncobrief/db` backend APIs** and is queued for Phase 05 remediation. All verified patient and caregiver continuity workflows in our submission (11-locale voice reminders, caregiver consent sync/revocation, and HMAC-signed two-way attendance webhooks) run on the **verified Next.js web platform (`/patients/[id]/continuity`)**.

#### Q15: *"If selected for a hospital pilot with the National Cancer Grid, how will you prove measurable impact in 60–90 days?"*
- **Answer:** Our database schema and `/analytics` service (`packages/db/src/services/analytics.ts`) already capture the exact timestamps and denominators needed for a 90-day pilot without manual tallying:
  1. **Consultation Readiness Rate:** Share of scheduled oncology OPD patients in `readiness_band = 'ready'` at check-in (`record_readiness_snapshot`, pilot target `>= 80%`).
  2. **Pre-Consult Chart-Prep Time:** Active time from first record review to `approvePacketAction` (`consultation_packet.approved_at`), benchmarked against Days 1–30 manual baseline.
  3. **Provenance & Safety Catch Rate:** Number of cross-facility contradictions caught and resolved (`conflict_set`) and unanchored AI candidates rejected (`extraction_candidate.validation_status = 'rejected'`).
  4. **OPD Slot Backfill & Attendance Confirmation:** Vacant slots backfilled by `ready` waitlisted patients (`opd_slot_event`) and confirmed attendance rate (`patient_message.attendance_status = 'confirmed_attendance'`), exportable via `/api/v1/analytics/export?format=csv`.
