# Phase 5 Execution Checklist: Cancer Patient and Caregiver Voice Continuity, WCAG Accessibility, Mobile Remediation, and Final System Integration

Goal: Implement Phase 5 deliverables, conduct Phase 4 safety review, and deliver V4 release-readiness gap audit.

## Architectural Pillars
1. **Caregiver Network & Lifecycle**: Persisted caregiver contacts with explicit consent provenance, preferences, and revocation handling under forced RLS.
2. **Multilingual Voice & Sarvam Bulbul v3**: 11 supported locales (1 English + 10 Indic languages), server-side credentials, short-lived delivery, privacy caching, and honest browser/synthetic fallbacks.
3. **Administrative Attendance Confirmation**: Two-way administrative responses (confirmed, cannot attend, needs assistance, no response) without autonomous clinical decisions.
4. **WCAG 2.2 AA & Mobile Remediation**: Contrast remediation, semantic HTML5 landmarks, form labeling, focus management, and responsive 375px mobile viewports.
5. **Audit Redaction Hardening**: Expand redaction of reasons, filenames, phone numbers, and reminder text to prevent PHI leakage.
6. **Phase 4 Safety Audit**: Independent review of de-identification, citations, patient handouts, and FHIR/mCODE exports.
7. **End-to-End Verification**: Monorepo verification (`pnpm verify`), production builds, and comprehensive Playwright browser E2E tests.
