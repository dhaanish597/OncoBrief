## 1. Scope Status Categories

In accordance with strict project rules, the previous `FUTURE_SCOPE` category has been eliminated. Every requirement is mapped into one of five definitive statuses:

1. **`BUILD_NOW`**: Concrete software capability, test, or adapter ready for immediate implementation in the active repository or dedicated worktree.
2. **`ALREADY_IMPLEMENTED_AND_VERIFIED`**: Verified working in current codebase with automated test evidence and non-diagnostic compliance.
3. **`EXTERNAL_CREDENTIAL_OR_APPROVAL_BLOCKED`**: Architectural interfaces, local fallbacks, sandbox harnesses, and contract tests are implemented now; live production traffic is bounded until hospital/telecom/NHA regulatory credentials and agreements are issued.
4. **`SAFETY_OR_HACKATHON_PROHIBITED`**: Autonomous diagnosis, treatment prescription, clinical risk scoring, or ungrounded generative inference strictly forbidden by clinical safety boundaries (`packages/domain/src/policy/clinical-boundary.ts`) and Health-a-thon rules.
5. **`DUPLICATE_OR_SUPERSEDED`**: Absorbed into or replaced by a more comprehensive, higher-precision implementation task.
