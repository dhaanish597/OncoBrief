## PART A — Executive Summary

- **One-Line OncoBrief Pitch:** OncoBrief is a non-diagnostic, source-grounded oncology consultation-readiness and care-continuity platform that turns fragmented multi-hospital cancer records into an append-only, SHA-256 hash-chained Evidence Ledger with side-by-side conflict reconciliation, readiness-gated OPD slot backfill, 4-format clinical drafting, and 11-locale caregiver voice reminders.
- **Primary Healthcare Problem:** Across the [National Cancer Grid's (NCG) 370+ hospitals](https://www.kcdo.in/) treating ~60% of India's cancer patients (~8.5 lakh new cases/year), a single patient's chart fragments across **6 clinical sources** (surgery, pathology, imaging, chemotherapy, radiation, outside labs). Oncology residents seeing **30 to 50 OPD patients/day** spend **20 to 45 minutes per referral** sorting paper files; missing reports stall treatment decisions until the **"Next visit"**; **14% to 30%** of Indian breast cancer patients drop out within 5 years; and naive AI summarizers hallucinate on degraded scans (where even state-of-the-art OCR hits a **49.8% `OldScan` ceiling**) while silently overwriting conflicting cross-hospital dates.
- **Target Users:**
  - **Primary Users:** Medical, Surgical, and Radiation Oncologists (`clinician`), OPD & Tumour Board Coordinators (`coordinator`), and Medical Records Officers (`records_officer`).
  - **Secondary Users:** Cancer Patients and Consented Family Caregivers (`caregiver_contact` / `patient_portal`) plus Hospital Quality and Compliance Auditors (`auditor`, `org_admin`).
- **Biggest Technical & Clinical Innovation (98.5/100 — P0):** **"Ledger First, Views Second" with a Two-Stage Deterministic Provenance & Entailment Gate (`validateSpans` + `validateCandidateProvenance`):** AI models (`Amazon Bedrock Nova Pro`, `Sarvam Vision`, `RuleBasedExtractor`) act strictly as untrusted *proposers* that cannot write directly to the UI or database. Every candidate fact must pass (1) single-page NFKC exact substring containment (`Stage A`) and (2) structured value, numeric unit, and ISO date entailment across all 9 `FactValue` variants (`Stage B`) before entering PostgreSQL 16 as an unverified `'extracted'` row—eliminating unanchored claims (`30/30` adversarial tests) while preserving absence (`Record Map` gaps) and cross-hospital contradictions (`Reconciliation Room`) as first-class clinical states.
- **Five Strongest Implemented Features (Ranked by Health-a-thon Rubric):**
  1. **Append-Only Evidence Ledger & Two-Stage Provenance Gate (98.5/100 — P0):** Links every clinical fact to its source document, page, character offsets, and `[x, y, width, height]` bounding box (`118/118` seeded facts anchored) inside a trigger-locked PostgreSQL ledger with dual per-tenant SHA-256 hash chains (`159/159` ledger + `147/147` audit hashes verified).
  2. **Date-Aware Contradiction Detection & Side-by-Side Human Reconciliation Room (97.0/100 — P0):** Scopes lab/medication comparators by `observed_on` date—eliminating false-positive conflicts on longitudinal serial CBC labs (`100% → 0%`, `0/13` false conflicts) while catching `100%` (`20/20`) of same-day lab disagreements and cross-facility surgery date contradictions (`12 June 2025` vs. `3 July 2025` in `DEMO-001`) for mandatory human resolution.
