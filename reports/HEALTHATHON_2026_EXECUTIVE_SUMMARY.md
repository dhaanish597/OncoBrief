## 1. Top 10 Strongest Implemented Capabilities (Verified in Code & Tests)

1. **Cryptographically Hash-Chained, Append-Only Evidence Ledger (`packages/db/migrations/0002_schema.sql`, `0003_policies_grants.sql`, `packages/db/src/services/ledger.ts`):** Database-level `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE` permissions and `oncobrief_block_mutation()` triggers guarantee that `evidence_fact`, `ledger_entry`, and `audit_event` cannot be mutated or deleted even by privileged application queries, with per-org SHA-256 hash chaining (`verifyChain`).
2. **14 Oncology-Specific Administrative Fact Schemas & Conflict Comparators (`packages/domain/src/vocab/fact-types.ts`, `packages/domain/src/conflict/comparators.ts`):** Automatically detects cross-facility contradictions across pathology dates, TNM staging strings, histology, discordant ER/PR/HER2 biomarker receptor statuses, and >5% same-day numeric lab divergences.
3. **1-Page Consultation Readiness Brief & Longitudinal Lab Flowsheet (`packages/domain/src/journey/longitudinal-review.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/EvidenceViewContainer.tsx`):** Synthesizes verified disease identity, receptor badges, systemic/radiation therapy history, serial oncology lab trends (`Hb`, `WBC`, `ANC`, `Platelets`, `Creatinine`, `CEA`, `CA 15-3`, `CA-125`) with `↑`/`↓` >10% delta markers, and interval changes since the last consultation.
4. **2-Click Batch Fact Verification (`packages/db/src/services/ledger.ts:verifyEvidenceBatch`, `apps/web/src/components/BatchVerificationModal.tsx`):** Allows clinicians and records officers to review and verify up to 25 high-confidence extracted facts with verbatim source quotes and page citations in a single transaction with `FOR UPDATE` row locking.
5. **Visual Bounding-Box Source Provenance Viewer (`apps/web/src/components/SourcePaneView.tsx`, `apps/web/src/app/(app)/patients/[id]/sources/[documentId]/page.tsx`):** Every extracted fact links to normalized `(bbox_x, bbox_y, bbox_w, bbox_h)` coordinates rendered directly over the source document page.
6. **Hard Runtime & Static Clinical Safety Boundary (`packages/domain/src/policy/clinical-boundary.ts`, `tools/check-clinical-boundary.ts`):** Deterministically intercepts and refuses requests for autonomous cancer staging inference, prognosis estimation, treatment selection, or chemotherapy dosing in Ask OncoBrief and CI checks.
7. **Deterministic Waitlist Backfill Ranking Engine (`packages/domain/src/flow/slot-optimizer.ts`, `packages/db/src/services/opd-flow.ts`):** Ranks waitlisted patients for vacated OPD slots using transparent administrative readiness criteria (`ready` +40, `almost_ready` +15, operational priority tag +10..25, wait days +2/day) with zero clinical severity inference.
8. **Source-Grounded Continuity Messaging & Instant Consent Revocation Gate (`packages/db/src/services/messages.ts`, `packages/db/src/services/caregivers.ts`, `packages/db/src/services/abdm.ts`):** Every variable in a patient/caregiver message is linked to a verified `evidence_fact` or `admin_task`; `checkMessageGate()` blocks delivery or TTS synthesis the instant a source fact is superseded or ABDM/caregiver consent is revoked.
9. **Multi-Tenant PostgreSQL Row-Level Security Failing Closed (`packages/db/migrations/0003_policies_grants.sql`, `0011_v4_oncology_enhancement.sql`):** 37 core and V4 tables enforce both `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` and `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` scoped to `current_setting('app.org_id', true)::uuid`.
10. **Comprehensive V3/V4 Automated Verification Suite (`packages/domain/src/__tests__/v4-domain.test.ts`, `packages/db/src/__tests__/integration.test.ts`, `apps/web/e2e/v4-workflows.spec.ts`):** 199 unit and real-PostgreSQL integration tests plus 10 Playwright E2E browser specs verify tenant isolation, hash-chain integrity, conflict reconciliation, batch verification, OPD slot concurrency, studio citation gates, and caregiver revocation.

## 2. Top 10 Missing, Broken, or Simulated Features (Verified Gaps)

1. **[BROKEN — P0] Workspace Readiness Counter & Filter Regression (`apps/web/src/app/(app)/workspace/page.tsx:107, 189-196`):** Milestone A rewrote `/workspace` to filter by `readinessBand === 'complete'` (and dropdown values `'complete' | 'high' | 'medium' | 'low' | 'incomplete' | 'none'`), whereas the PostgreSQL `readiness_band` enum and domain logic emit `'ready' | 'almost_ready' | 'needs_records' | 'blocked'` (or `'gaps'`). As a result, the main dashboard always reports **`0 ready for consult`** (even though `DEMO-003` is `ready`) and every readiness filter option returns **0 patients**.
2. **[BROKEN — P0] Milestone B Demo Fixtures Never Seed (`packages/db/src/seed.ts:1148-1150, 1242-1350`):** `bootstrapDemo()` returns early on initial `seed()` (skipping Milestone B tables) and on subsequent runs queries `WHERE email = 'ananya.mukhopadhyay@rci.invalid'` (line 1244), whereas the seeded clinician email is `'dr.ananya@rci.demo'`. Consequently, `docMukhopadhyay` is always `undefined`, and **zero** Milestone B `clinic_session`, `appointment`, `encounter`, or `tumor_board_meeting` rows are ever seeded.
3. **[BROKEN — P0] Clinical Boundary & Ledger Contradiction in Milestone B Forms (`apps/web/src/app/(app)/tumor-board/page.tsx:246, 354`, `apps/web/src/app/(app)/patients/[id]/encounters/[encounterId]/page.tsx`):** `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/milestone-a.md` state that *"No form permits manual input of cancer diagnosis, TNM staging, or treatment regimens. All clinical facts must emerge exclusively from uploaded medical source documents through the immutable Evidence Ledger."* Yet Milestone B added mutable free-text inputs for working TNM staging (`placeholder="e.g. cT2aN1M0 Stage IIB"`), clinical assessments, and treatment recommendations (`placeholder="Board treatment recommendations, trial eligibility..."`) outside the Evidence Ledger.
4. **[PARTIAL — P0] Split-Brain Scheduling Models (`packages/db/src/services/opd-flow.ts` vs `packages/db/src/services/appointments.ts`):** V4 OPD Flow (`/workspace?view=opd`) uses `opd_appointment_slot`, while Milestone B (`/appointments`) uses `appointment`. Although migration `0019` added a nullable `appointment_id` column to `opd_appointment_slot`, neither service updates the other—checking in a patient on `/appointments` leaves them un-checked-in on `/workspace?view=opd`.
5. **[MISSING — P1] Planned `WorkflowStateGraph<TState>` Agent Orchestrator (`packages/domain/src/orchestration/workflow-graph.ts`):** Completely absent from the repository. Ask OncoBrief (`apps/web/src/lib/assistant/orchestrator.ts`) uses regex arrays (`classify.ts`) + a 1-token LLM classifier fallback, followed by hardcoded `if/else` database queries and a single-turn synthesis call (or deterministic string template fallback).
6. **[PARTIAL — P1] Synchronous Web Uploads Bypass LLM Extraction & Stage B Provenance (`apps/web/src/lib/actions.ts:472`, `packages/db/src/services/ledger.ts:199`):** `extractorPort()` in `actions.ts` hardcodes `new RuleBasedExtractor()` (ignoring `EXTRACTION_DRIVER=llm`), and `insertFact()` in `ledger.ts` calls `validateSpans()` (Stage A) but never calls `validateCandidateProvenance()` (Stage B substring containment).
7. **[SIMULATED — P2] Hardcoded 4.0-Second Fake Progress Stepper in Upload UI (`apps/web/src/components/DocumentUploadForm.tsx:166-197`):** `runStepSequence()` executes six sequential `setTimeout` delays (`700+800+700+600+600+600 = 4000ms`) displaying *"Extracting tabular text spans with Sarvam Vision…"* even when `OCR_DRIVER=local-pdf` and Sarvam Vision is never invoked.
8. **[PARTIAL — P1] Productivity Studio Exact-Clause Edit Lock (`packages/domain/src/studio/draft-generator.ts:413-485`, `apps/web/src/lib/studio-validation.ts`):** `revalidateStudioMarkdownCitations()` checks every edited line containing a citation against exact normalized clauses of the auto-generated template. 9. **[PARTIAL — P1] Minimal Guideline Corpus & Broken `[G#]` Citation Deep-Link (`packages/db/src/seed.ts:1032-1065`, `apps/web/src/app/(app)/patients/[id]/sources/page.tsx`):** Only 1 synthetic SOP with 2 spans is seeded; there is no UI/API to upload new guideline PDFs; and clicking a `[G#]` source card in Ask OncoBrief navigates to `/patients/[id]/sources?guideline=<id>&page=<n>`, which `sources/page.tsx` ignores.
